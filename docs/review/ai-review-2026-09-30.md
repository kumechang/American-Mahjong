## Changed in `src/content/learn.ts`
Only points that are certain, or that make the text less absolute:
- **Scoring:** removed the "minimum points before you can go out" sentence
  (not an NMJL rule); added who pays what (discarder pays double, self-drawn
  win: all three pay double, jokerless doubling) with a "your card and group
  tell you which apply" caveat; concealed hands rewritten around the card's
  "C" marking.
- **Charleston:** named the directions (right, across, left; then left,
  across, right), made the optional second round clear, added the blind pass
  and the courtesy pass, and stated that jokers can't be passed.
- **Calling:** a discard can also be called to complete a whole hand; a
  discarded joker can't be called; Mahjong beats a pung/kong call.
- **Rules:** play goes counterclockwise; new short "Dead hands" section.
- **Jokers:** swap wording is now one-for-one and works on your own
  exposures too; Terms and Jokers now agree (never a single or a pair).
- **Terms:** added "Quint"; joker/kong/call/wall-game entries aligned.
- **Wall game:** no longer says "the same dealer deals again" (unverified);
  now says to ask the group.
- **Wording:** removed "having a moment", "coat of paint", "jokers freely",
  a circular sentence in the Beginner Guide; the first-line "14 tiles"
  claim (only the dealer starts with 14).
- **Etiquette:** added "say the tile's name when you discard" (most groups).

## Still open — for a human expert (Questions)
1. After a wall game, does the same dealer deal again, or does the deal pass?
2. Can a hand still count as concealed if it wins on a discard for the last tile?
3. Is there a last-tile / last-discard rule that decides when a wall game is declared?
4. Do the card's C (concealed) and X (exposed) values work as the Scoring
   section describes?
5. Does the card mark jokerless hands explicitly, or only by structure?
6. Is there a dealer bonus (the reviewer believes not)?
7. Are the Etiquette conventions right (no taking back a discard, not
   discussing your own hand, claim promptly)? Currently not stated.
8. Exact blind-pass wording: which pass in each Charleston may be blind,
   and how many tiles?

## Still open — for the English editor
- **Terminology decision:** use "American Mahjong" for the game and
  "Mah Jongg" only in proper names ("National Mah Jongg League", listing
  titles). Several intros mix the two.
- **NMJL:** spell out "National Mah Jongg League" once per page, then "NMJL".
- **City intro repetition:** about 17 intros open with "[City]'s listings
  center on…", about 16 use "If you are new…", and about 20 end with
  "Check the … before you go". Suggested alternatives: (1) lead with the
  reader's goal ("Want to learn first? … Already play? …"); (2) lead with one
  concrete useful fact (day, place type, price, free option); (3) two-part
  "Learn / Play" shape and drop generic closing lines. A rewrite of all
  intros should wait for the editor's pass to avoid doing it twice.
- Em dashes are heavy across the Learn guides; consider trimming.
