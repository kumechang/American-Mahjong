### atlanta

Atlanta's listings lean toward lessons and social play. Le Mahj Club runs 101 and 102 beginner lessons and casual open-play socials around Alpharetta, Roswell and Milton, while Mahj Studios in Buckhead is a membership club with beginner lessons, guided play and socials. Several independent instructors also teach American Mahjong across the metro. If you're new, a beginner lesson is the clearest first step.

### austin

Austin mixes leagues, a studio and pop-up lessons. Austin Mahjong Social offers classes, private lessons and seasonal leagues. Mahj House Austin is a studio in West Lake Hills with a beginner series, open play and social nights. itsamahjing hosts lessons and themed pop-up events around town. Beginner-friendly options are marked on each listing, so you can start with a class and move into league or open play once you know the game.

### baltimore

Baltimore's listings span the wider metro. The Tiles Social Club hosts open play and lessons at cafes and other public venues, Severna Park Community Center runs a beginner MAHJ 101 course and guided open play, and Howard County Library holds a meet-up for all levels. Iris Pressman and other local teachers offer private and group lessons. Since play is spread across venues, check the calendar and how far you will need to travel before your first visit.
