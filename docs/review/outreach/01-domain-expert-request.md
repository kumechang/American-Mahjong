## 8 questions we especially want answered
(Ask these in a follow-up message or on the call — they are the open items
from an earlier AI fact-check.)
1. After a wall game (nobody wins), does the same dealer deal again, or does
   the deal pass?
2. Can a hand still count as concealed if it wins on a discard for the last tile?
3. Is there a last-tile or last-discard rule that decides when a wall game
   is declared?
4. Do the card's C (concealed) and X (exposed) values work the way our
   Scoring page describes?
5. Does the card mark jokerless hands explicitly, or only by how the hand is built?
6. Is there a dealer bonus in American Mahjong?
7. Which etiquette conventions do you consider universal (calling out
   discards, no taking back a discard, not discussing your hand, claiming a
   tile promptly)?
8. Blind pass: which pass in each Charleston may be blind, and how many tiles
   may be passed blind?
