# Request to a US English editor (send as an email or a freelance job post)

**Subject / job title:** Copy edit for a consumer website, ~7,000 words (fixed price)

Hi [Name],

I run a small consumer website that helps adults learn American Mahjong and
find local clubs and lessons (mahjong-map.com). I'm looking for a native US
English editor to review the site's text before I promote it.

**Scope (about 7,000 words in two files)**
1. **Learn guides** — 8 short beginner guides (about 2,600 words).
2. **City introductions** — one short paragraph for each of about 50 city
   pages (about 4,200 words).
3. A one-page **voice guide** describing the tone the site aims for.

**The audience** is mostly American adults, many 40 to 80, and many are
brand-new to the game. The voice is a warm, encouraging friend who is
teaching at her kitchen table: clear, short sentences, second person
("you"), about a 7th–8th grade reading level, no gimmicks or puns.

**What I need**
- Wording that reads unnaturally to a native US reader, awkward or
  ambiguous sentences, and tone that drifts (too salesy, stiff, patronizing).
- Terminology and capitalization choices, with your recommendation. (For
  example: "American Mahjong" for the game, "Mah Jongg" only in proper names
  like the National Mah Jongg League; how to introduce "NMJL"; "open play".)
- The **city introductions** repeat the same shape ("X's listings center
  on… / If you are new… / Check the … before you go"). Please point out the
  repetition and suggest 2–3 alternative structures, then rewrite five
  sample intros in those structures so I can see the effect.
- Claims that sound like guarantees ("the easiest way", "the best place").

**Not needed**
- Checking mahjong rules (a separate expert does that). If a rule looks
  wrong to you, just flag it.
- Proofreading of listings, prices or business names.

**How to deliver**
Use the attached spreadsheet (`feedback-template.csv`) or Google Docs
suggestions. Please mark each item High (reader would stumble or be misled),
Medium (noticeably unnatural) or Low (polish).

**Timeline and budget**
- Turnaround: about one week.
- Budget: [$___ fixed price — decide before sending].
- Optional first step: a paid sample (the Charleston and Scoring guides,
  about 600 words) so we can both see if the fit is right.

If you're interested, please send: your rate or fixed-price quote, a link
or two to consumer-facing writing you've edited, and when you could start.

Thank you,
[Your name]
mahjong-map.com
