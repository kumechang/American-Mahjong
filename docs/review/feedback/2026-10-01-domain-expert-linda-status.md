## Applied in `src/content/learn.ts`
- **Calling:** sextets added; no calling a discard for a single or pair unless it
  gives Mahjong; after calling you discard, you don't draw; timing; Mahjong beats
  any call, and ties go to the player whose turn is next.
- **Setup:** the wall is two tiles high and 19 long.
- **Terms:** Mahjong ends the *round*; new entries Flower, Soap, East, Dead Hand,
  Blind Pass, Courtesy Pass, Singles and Pairs; Call mentions sextets.
- **Charleston:** a "no" skips only the second round (the courtesy pass still
  happens); courtesy pass: the smaller number wins, jokers can't be passed.
- **Scoring:** every hand is marked C or X; a C hand can still win on a discard
  for its last tile and stays concealed; C is not a bonus (points are the
  printed number); jokerless hands pay double except Singles and Pairs, and the
  doubles add up (self-drawn jokerless = four times); no standard dealer bonus.
- **Jokers:** the card has no special "no jokers" mark — Singles and Pairs hands
  are all singles and pairs; you may swap more than one joker per turn.
- **Etiquette:** naming each discard is a rule; no take-backs (beginner tables
  may allow it); no table talk about your own hand; call a discard promptly.
- **Dead hands:** softened ("your tile count is wrong, or you call Mahjong by
  mistake and lay your hand down… groups handle mistaken calls differently").
